## 1. Purpose

This document defines the official brand identity and usage rules for Kawalis China and KC Platform V2.

Its purpose is to preserve a consistent, recognizable, trustworthy identity across the public website, customer portal, internal platform, documents, presentations, social channels, campaigns, and approved third-party materials.

This document governs brand expression. Detailed interface components and interaction rules belong in `KC-V2-005 — DESIGN_SYSTEM.md`.

---

## 2. Brand Status

The Kawalis China visual identity is **frozen**. Implementation must use the approved identity rather than reinterpret it.

The following require explicit owner approval:

- Changing the logo or KC symbol
- Changing official colors
- Changing official typefaces
- Replacing the official Arabic or English tagline
- Introducing a new primary visual direction
- Publishing an unapproved logo variant

Technical adaptations are permitted only when they preserve the approved identity and are required for accessibility, rendering, responsive use, or a supported medium.

**Platform name:** KC Platform V2

### Official Taglines

**English:** China Decoded for the World

## 4. Brand Positioning

Kawalis China is a professional sourcing and trade-operations partner that helps customers navigate China through a structured, transparent, and trackable process.

The brand must feel:

- Knowledgeable without arrogance
- Premium without unnecessary extravagance
- Professional without coldness
- Chinese in context without visual cliché
- Clear without oversimplifying important decisions
- Helpful without making unsupported promises

Kawalis China is not positioned as:

- A general marketplace
- A public supplier directory
- A discount broker
- A replacement for Alibaba
- A fully automated sourcing agent
- A generic SaaS product

---

## 5. Brand Promise

Kawalis China helps customers understand and manage sourcing from China with greater clarity, control, and confidence.

Every branded experience should reinforce:

- Clear process
- Responsible guidance
- Controlled customer visibility
- Real operational evidence
- Professional follow-through
- Respect for commercial confidentiality

The brand must never imply guaranteed prices, guaranteed suppliers, guaranteed quality, guaranteed delivery dates, or risk-free importing unless a specific written agreement supports that claim.

---

## 6. Brand Audience

Primary audiences include:

- Small and medium-sized businesses
- Retailers and e-commerce sellers
- Importers and entrepreneurs
- Startup and project owners
- Restaurants, cafés, and hospitality businesses
- Companies seeking packaging, furniture, equipment, private-label products, or production solutions

The audience may have limited sourcing experience. Communication must reduce uncertainty without hiding real tradeoffs, responsibilities, or risks.

---

## 7. Brand Personality

The official personality is:

### Clear

Explain the next step, expected inputs, visible status, and responsibility. Avoid vague language and unexplained trade terminology.

### Trustworthy

Use verified facts, approved evidence, and realistic expectations. Trust must come from process quality rather than unsupported numbers.

### Experienced

Communicate practical knowledge of China sourcing, suppliers, inspection, purchasing, and logistics without exaggeration.

### Calm

Use confident, measured communication. Avoid urgency tactics, visual noise, and aggressive sales language.

### Premium

Quality is expressed through restraint, precision, typography, spacing, and detail—not decorative excess.

### Human

The platform supports real customers and operational teams. Language should remain approachable, respectful, and useful.

---

## 8. Brand Voice

The brand voice must be:

- Direct
- Professional
- Reassuring
- Specific
- Respectful
- Operationally accurate
- Easy to understand

### Voice Rules

- Lead with the user's need or next action.
- Prefer short, concrete sentences.
- Explain unfamiliar trade terms when they first appear.
- Distinguish confirmed facts from estimates.
- State limitations and dependencies honestly.
- Use active voice where practical.
- Avoid inflated corporate language.
- Avoid literal translations that sound unnatural in Arabic.

### Examples

Preferred:

> أرسل تفاصيل المنتج، وسيراجع فريق التوريد الطلب قبل التواصل مع الموردين.

Avoid:

> سنوفر لك أفضل مورد وأقل سعر مضمون في الصين.

Preferred:

> عرض السعر قيد المراجعة الداخلية، ولن يظهر للعميل حتى اعتماده.

Avoid:

> عرضك المثالي أصبح جاهزًا فورًا.

## 9. Arabic and English Usage

Arabic is the primary product language and must receive first-class editorial and visual treatment.

### Arabic

- Use Modern Standard Arabic with a clear, natural Gulf-friendly tone.
- Avoid unnecessary dialect, slang, and overly formal bureaucratic phrasing.
- Preserve correct right-to-left reading order.
- Translate meaning and intent rather than word order.
- Use Arabic terminology first; include an English trade term when it materially helps recognition.

### English

- Use clear international business English.
- Avoid idioms that do not translate well.
- Keep terminology consistent with the Arabic experience.
- Use sentence case for interface copy unless a proper name requires otherwise.

### Mixed Content

Mixed Arabic and English content must preserve readable direction, punctuation, product codes, currencies, units, and identifiers. Technical IDs and tracking references must not be localized in a way that changes their value.

---

## 10. Visual Direction

The approved direction is:

> **Editorial Luxury + Modern Trade Platform**

The visual identity combines editorial restraint with the clarity and reliability required for a trade-operations platform.

### Principles

- RTL first
- Mobile first
- Calm and premium
- Strong typographic hierarchy
- Generous, purposeful spacing
- Clear operational information
- Subtle Chinese cultural references
- Minimal decorative noise
- Evidence-based trust

### Avoid

- Generic SaaS gradients and illustrations
- Excessive red or gold
- Crowded dashboards
- Decorative Chinese symbols without meaning
- Neon colors
- Heavy shadows and glossy effects
- Unnecessary animation
- Stock imagery that reinforces stereotypes
- Unsupported trust counters or invented statistics

---

## 11. Official Color Palette

| Token | Hex | Primary role |
|---|---:|---|
| Luxury Gold | `#C9A24A` | Premium accents, selected highlights, restrained brand details |
| Chinese Red | `#CC2828` | Primary brand action, focal emphasis, important alerts where semantically correct |
| Charcoal | `#222222` | Primary text, dark surfaces, strong contrast |
| Ivory | `#F8F5EF` | Primary warm background and editorial canvas |

### Color Meaning

**Luxury Gold** communicates heritage, value, and considered quality. It is an accent, not a replacement for readable text or a universal status color.

**Chinese Red** expresses brand identity and decisive action. It must not dominate every surface or represent success merely because it is a brand color.

**Charcoal** provides authority, readability, and restraint.

**Ivory** provides warmth and a premium editorial foundation without the sterility of pure white.

### Usage Rules

- Maintain accessible text and control contrast.
- Do not use gold for small body text on ivory or white backgrounds.
- Do not communicate status using color alone.
- Use semantic colors for success, warning, and error in the Design System; they must harmonize with the brand palette.
- Tints, shades, and opacity variants may be created systematically in the Design System without redefining the official colors.
- Do not replace official colors with visually similar alternatives in brand assets.

## 12. Typography

### Arabic Typeface

**IBM Plex Sans Arabic** is the approved Arabic typeface.

It should be used for:

- Arabic interface text
- Arabic headings
- Arabic documents and presentations
- Arabic marketing content

### English Typeface

**Montserrat** is the approved English typeface.

It should be used for:

- English interface text
- English headings
- Latin numbers and supporting English copy when appropriate
- English brand materials

### Gotham

Gotham may be used only when a valid license and approved font files are available. It must not be downloaded, bundled, or distributed without documented licensing.

### Fallbacks

Implementation must define safe system fallbacks that preserve readability when a web font fails. Fallback selection belongs in the Design System.

### Typography Rules

- Readability takes precedence over decorative effect.
- Avoid excessive weights and condensed text.
- Use a consistent hierarchy.
- Do not stretch, skew, outline, or artificially modify typefaces.
- Test Arabic diacritics, punctuation, numerals, and mixed-language strings.
- Body text must remain comfortable on small screens.

## 14. Logo Usage Rules

### Required

- Preserve the original proportions.
- Preserve the approved colors.
- Use the correct light or dark variant for the background.
- Maintain sufficient clear space.
- Keep the logo legible at the intended size.
- Use a high-quality vector or appropriate raster asset.
- Preserve the Chinese seal in official variants where it is part of the approved artwork.

### Prohibited

- Stretching or compressing the logo
- Rotating or skewing the logo
- Recoloring individual parts
- Adding shadows, outlines, glow, bevel, or gradients
- Redrawing or altering the KC symbol
- Changing the relationship between symbol, name, seal, and tagline
- Cropping the logo unintentionally
- Placing the logo on a visually noisy or low-contrast background
- Using an unofficial lockup
- Animating the logo without explicit approval

---

## 15. Clear Space and Minimum Size

The approved logo asset should define its own clear-space unit when a formal brand board is available.

Until exact measurements are documented:

- Maintain clear space on every side of at least the visual height of a key internal KC mark element.
- Do not allow text, borders, icons, or imagery to enter the clear area.
- Do not render a logo version so small that the name, seal, or symbol becomes unclear.
- Use the simplified approved icon or favicon at very small sizes rather than forcing the full logo.

Exact pixel and print minimums must be recorded after the final source logo assets are available and inspected. Implementers must not invent permanent dimensions.

---

## 16. Logo Backgrounds

### Ivory or Light Background

Use the approved primary or dark-text logo version with sufficient contrast.

### Charcoal or Dark Background

Use the approved light or gold variant intended for dark surfaces.

### Chinese Red Background

Use only an approved high-contrast logo variant. Avoid red logo elements that disappear into the background.

### Photography

Place the logo only on a calm area with dependable contrast. When necessary, use an approved solid container or overlay rather than modifying the logo.

---

## 17. Brand Imagery

Imagery should communicate real sourcing work, product detail, factories, inspection, trade movement, Chinese locations, and human expertise.

### Preferred

- Authentic operational photography
- Close product and material details
- Real inspection and sourcing evidence
- Clean architectural or trade-context photography
- Natural human moments with appropriate consent
- Calm composition and realistic color treatment

### Avoid

- Unverified supplier or factory claims
- Stereotypical or tourist-only depictions of China
- Overused landmark imagery as the main brand language
- Artificially perfect stock-business handshakes
- Misleading luxury imagery unrelated to the service
- Images containing confidential customer, supplier, document, or product information
- AI-generated imagery presented as operational evidence

### Image Disclosure

Illustrative or AI-generated images must not be represented as proof of real factories, inspections, suppliers, shipments, or customer outcomes.

---

## 18. Chinese Cultural Elements

Chinese cultural references must remain subtle, respectful, and contextually relevant.

Approved direction may include:

- Restrained seal-inspired details
- Editorial line patterns
- Architectural geometry
- Thoughtful use of red and gold
- Authentic location or trade photography

Avoid:

- Decorative symbols whose meaning has not been verified
- Random Chinese characters
- Cultural caricatures
- Excessive lantern, dragon, or pagoda motifs
- Mixing unrelated Asian visual traditions
- Using sacred, political, or culturally sensitive imagery casually

Any Chinese-language text used as a visual or message must be verified by a competent speaker before publication.

---

## 19. Iconography and Illustration

Iconography should be simple, functional, consistent, and compatible with RTL interfaces.

### Rules

- Use one coherent icon family in the product.
- Prefer outline or restrained solid icons with consistent weight.
- Icons must support text rather than replace unclear labels.
- Directional icons must mirror appropriately in RTL.
- Brand colors should be used selectively.
- Illustrations must follow the editorial, premium, trade-focused direction.

Do not build a decorative illustration system before a confirmed need exists.

---

## 20. Layout and Composition

Branded layouts should use:

- Clear hierarchy
- Strong alignment
- Purposeful whitespace
- Restrained color blocks
- Editorial rhythm
- Readable measures
- Mobile-first composition

The interface must not place decoration ahead of task clarity. Customer requests, quotations, documents, and status updates require functional clarity first.

Detailed spacing, grids, breakpoints, radii, and component composition belong in the Design System.

---

## 21. Trust and Evidence

Trust must be supported by verifiable process evidence.

Preferred trust signals include:

- Clear sourcing stages
- Real inspection evidence with permission
- Transparent status explanations
- Approved customer testimonials
- Verified certifications or partnerships
- Clear privacy and service terms
- Accurate company and contact information
- Real response expectations

Prohibited trust practices include:

- Placeholder or invented statistics
- Unsupported claims such as "best," "guaranteed," or "number one"
- Fake reviews or logos
- Unverified factory counts, request counts, or success rates
- Presenting stock or generated imagery as actual evidence

Any numeric claim must have a named owner, source, calculation method, and review date.

---

## 22. Customer Status Language

Customer-facing status terms should be calm, simple, and action-oriented.

Each status should explain:

- What is happening
- Whether the customer needs to act
- What the next expected step is

Internal status terminology must not appear automatically in customer interfaces. Internal risks, supplier discussions, costs, and draft decisions remain restricted.

The final status vocabulary and mappings will be defined in a separate approved catalog.

---

## 23. Calls to Action

Primary calls to action should use clear verbs and realistic outcomes.

Preferred examples:

- ابدأ طلب توريد
- تابع طلبك
- أكمل بيانات المنتج
- راجع عرض السعر
- اطلب توضيحًا

Avoid:

- Ambiguous labels such as "انطلق" when the action is unclear
- Pressure language
- False urgency
- Promises that exceed the workflow
- Multiple competing primary actions on the same view

---

## 24. Accessibility

Brand implementation must not reduce accessibility.

Requirements include:

- Sufficient contrast
- Readable text at mobile sizes
- Visible keyboard focus
- Text alternatives for meaningful images
- Logo alt text appropriate to context
- No meaning conveyed by color alone
- Reduced-motion support where animation exists
- Clear Arabic and English reading order
- Accessible documents and presentations when practical

If a brand treatment conflicts with essential accessibility, the implementation must use the closest compliant treatment and document the decision.

---

## 25. Application Across Channels

### Website and Platform

Use the approved palette, typefaces, logo variants, voice, and RTL behavior. Operational clarity takes priority over marketing decoration.

### Documents

Proposals, quotations, inspection reports, and official PDFs must use approved branding without obscuring data or legal information.

### Presentations

Use editorial restraint, large readable typography, and evidence-based claims. Avoid overcrowded slides.

### Social Media

Maintain recognizable colors, typography, and tone. Social content may be more conversational but must remain accurate and respectful.

### Email and Messaging

Prioritize clarity, sender identity, next action, and safe links. Do not rely on visual branding alone to establish authenticity.

### Third Parties

Partners and vendors receive only approved assets and written usage rules. Permission to use the brand does not imply permission to alter it.

---

## 26. Brand Asset Management

Official assets should be maintained in a controlled directory or approved asset system.

Each asset should identify:

- Asset name
- Variant and intended background
- File format
- Version
- Approval status
- Owner

### Preferred Formats

- SVG for scalable digital logo assets where supported
- PNG for transparent raster use
- WebP or AVIF for optimized photography where appropriate
- PDF or suitable vector format for professional print exchange

Do not use screenshots of logos as production assets.

Source files, licenses, and approved exports must be preserved. Obsolete assets should be archived to prevent accidental reuse.

---

## 27. Governance and Approval

### Owner Authority

Kawalis China is the final authority for:

- Brand identity
- Logo variants
- Official colors
- Typefaces
- Taglines
- Major campaign direction
- Public partnerships and co-branding

### Review Required

Review is required for:

- New logo applications
- New permanent brand assets
- New primary campaign styles
- Co-branding
- Public numeric claims
- Chinese-language brand copy
- Material deviations required by a platform or medium

### Implementation Changes

Developers and designers may create tokens, responsive layouts, accessible fallbacks, and optimized asset formats when they preserve the approved identity. Material changes require documentation and owner approval.

---

## 28. Brand Quality Checklist

Before publication, confirm:

- The correct official logo variant is used.
- Logo proportions, color, clear space, and contrast are preserved.
- Official colors and typefaces are applied correctly.
- Arabic RTL behavior is correct.
- English and Arabic copy are natural and consistent.
- The tone is clear, calm, professional, and accurate.
- Claims and statistics are verified.
- Imagery is authentic or clearly illustrative.
- Cultural elements are respectful and verified.
- Accessibility requirements are met.
- No confidential data appears in visual materials.
- The artifact supports the platform's approved product direction.

---

## 29. Prohibited Brand Practices

- Modifying or redrawing the KC symbol
- Creating unapproved logo variants
- Changing official colors without approval
- Bundling unlicensed Gotham files
- Using fake testimonials, metrics, certificates, or partner logos
- Presenting generated imagery as operational evidence
- Using culturally inaccurate Chinese text or symbols
- Publishing confidential customer or supplier information
- Sacrificing accessibility for visual appearance
- Making guaranteed commercial claims without written support
- Turning the platform into a generic SaaS visual identity
